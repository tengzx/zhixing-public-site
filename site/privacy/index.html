Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Zhixing Time (“Zhixing,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) helps you track time, manage goals and actions, review your calendar, and plan an ideal day. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, when it is shared, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: nickname, preferred language, authentication credentials, and account identifiers. Passwords are sent over HTTPS and are intended to be stored by our authentication service as non-reversible password hashes, not as readable passwords.
  • Third-party sign-in information: if you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the Apple account identifier and identity token. Depending on your choices, Apple may also provide your name and email address.
  • Content you create: time records, event and app names, start and end times, categories, goals, actions, notes, ideal-day settings, and classification results.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens stored in the device Keychain, app language and preferences, and limited request data such as IP address, timestamps, and error information processed by network infrastructure for delivery, security, and troubleshooting.
  • Notification settings: the app may request permission to deliver local reminders. Reminder scheduling is handled on your device.

Zhixing does not use advertising SDKs, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, provide the app’s time-tracking and planning features, keep the iPhone and paired Apple Watch consistent, process optional automatic classification when you use it, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

3. Local storage, automatic classification, and backup

Ordinary app content is designed to remain available locally and offline. If you use the optional automatic-classification service, the event name and the names and identifiers of candidate categories are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to the OpenAI API so a category can be suggested. Our database stores a one-way hash of the event name, the classification result, model version, timing/status metadata, and any correction you submit; it does not store the raw event name from that request.

If iCloud Backup is available in your build and you use it, the latest seven rotating backups of your content are stored in your private iCloud database under your Apple account. Apple processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to provide features you choose:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit backup when enabled, device-to-Watch communication, and App Store distribution.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the authentication and classification service and its D1 database, and processes limited request and security-log information. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: receives the event name and candidate-category catalog only when you use optional automatic classification. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.
  • Legal and safety: information may be disclosed if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the service.

We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

5. Retention

  • Local content remains on your device until you delete it, close your account in the app, or remove the app and its data.
  • Account information, refresh-token hashes, classification-request metadata, and classification feedback are retained while your account is active and deleted when account closure is successfully completed, except for limited records we must retain for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or law.
  • Cloudflare Workers operational logs are retained for no more than 7 days under the platform’s current limits. Under OpenAI’s standard API retention, classification inputs and outputs may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, unless a legal requirement applies or a shorter approved retention setting is used.
  • When iCloud Backup is enabled, Zhixing keeps up to seven rolling backups. iCloud data remains under your Apple account and may also need to be managed through Apple’s iCloud settings.

6. Deletion and your choices

  • Close your account: in Zhixing, open Preferences → Other Settings → Close Account. After server confirmation, the app removes your account credentials, associated server data, and account-scoped local data. This action cannot be undone.
  • Delete individual content: use the deletion controls provided for records, goals, and actions.
  • Remove local data: close the account or delete the app from your device. Apple may retain system backups according to your device and iCloud settings.
  • Withdraw notification permission: use iOS Settings → Notifications → Zhixing Time.
  • Withdraw third-party sign-in access: manage Sign in with Apple in your Apple ID settings. Revoking access does not itself delete your Zhixing account; use Close Account for deletion.
  • Request help: email teng.tzx@gmail.com if you cannot access the in-app deletion control.

7. Children

Zhixing is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the user’s jurisdiction. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security and international processing

We use safeguards including HTTPS for network transport and the iOS Keychain for authentication tokens. No security measure is perfect. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to teng.tzx@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 28 日

知行（“我们”）用于时间追踪、目标与行动管理、日历复盘和理想日规划。本政策说明 App 处理哪些信息、处理目的、第三方共享、保留期限、删除方式与撤回授权方式。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 账户信息：昵称、首选语言、登录凭证与账户标识。密码通过 HTTPS 传输，并应由认证服务以不可逆哈希保存，而不是以可读明文保存。
  • 第三方登录信息：选择 Apple 登录时，我们会收到 Apple 账户标识和身份令牌；Apple 也可能根据你的选择提供姓名和电子邮箱。
  • 你创建的内容：时间记录、事件和 App 名称、开始与结束时间、分类、目标、行动、备注、理想日设置与自动分类结果。
  • 技术信息：保存在设备钥匙串中的登录令牌、App 语言和偏好，以及网络基础设施为传输、安全和排错处理的有限请求信息，例如 IP 地址、时间戳和错误信息。
  • 通知设置：App 可能请求发送本地提醒；提醒调度在设备上完成。

知行不使用广告 SDK，不跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你，也不出售个人信息。

2. 使用目的

我们仅将信息用于账户认证、提供时间追踪和规划功能、保持 iPhone 与配对 Apple Watch 状态一致、在你使用时执行可选自动分类、防止滥用、排查故障、处理支持请求和履行法律义务。

3. 本地存储、自动分类与备份

普通 App 内容以本地和离线可用为设计原则。如果你使用可选的自动分类服务，事件名称及候选分类的名称和标识会通过我们的 Cloudflare Worker 发送给 OpenAI API，以生成分类建议。我们的数据库只保存事件名称的单向哈希、分类结果、模型版本、耗时/状态信息及你提交的纠正，不保存该请求中的原始事件名称。

如果当前版本提供 iCloud 备份且你使用该功能，最近 7 份循环备份会存入你 Apple 账户下的私有 iCloud 数据库。Apple 依据其条款和隐私政策处理这些数据。

4. 第三方共享

  • Apple：用于 Apple 登录、启用时的 iCloud/CloudKit 备份、设备与 Apple Watch 通信及 App Store 分发。
  • Cloudflare：托管认证与分类服务及 D1 数据库，并处理有限的请求与安全日志信息。详见 Cloudflare 隐私政策。
  • OpenAI：仅在你使用可选自动分类时接收事件名称和候选分类目录。详见 OpenAI 隐私政策。
  • 法律与安全：法律要求或为保护用户、公众或服务而合理必要时，可能披露信息。

我们不会向数据经纪商或广告商共享个人信息。

5. 保留期限

  • 本地内容会保留到你主动删除、在 App 内完成注销账户，或移除 App 及其数据。
  • 账户信息、刷新令牌哈希、分类请求元数据和分类纠正在账户有效期间保留；账户注销成功后删除，但出于安全、防欺诈、争议处理或法律要求必须保留的有限记录除外。
  • 按 Cloudflare 当前平台限制，Workers 运行日志最长保留 7 天。按 OpenAI 标准 API 保留规则，分类输入和输出可能为防止滥用保留最多 30 天；法律要求或获批的更短保留配置除外。
  • 启用 iCloud 备份时，知行保留最多 7 份循环备份；iCloud 数据归属你的 Apple 账户，可能还需在 Apple 的 iCloud 设置中管理。

6. 删除与撤回授权

  • 注销账户：在知行中打开“偏好设置 → 其他设置 → 注销账户”。服务器确认后，App 会删除账户登录凭证、服务器关联数据和该账户的本地数据，且无法撤销。
  • 删除单项内容：使用记录、目标和行动页面提供的删除功能。
  • 删除本地数据：注销账户或从设备删除 App。Apple 仍可能根据设备和 iCloud 设置保留系统备份。
  • 撤回通知权限：前往“iOS 设置 → 通知 → Zhixing Time”。
  • 撤回第三方登录授权：在 Apple ID 设置中管理“通过 Apple 登录”。撤回授权不会自动删除知行账户；如需删除请使用“注销账户”。
  • 需要协助：如果无法使用 App 内注销功能，请发送邮件至 teng.tzx@gmail.com。

7. 儿童

知行不面向 13 岁以下儿童或用户所在地法律规定的同等最低年龄。如你认为儿童提供了个人信息，请联系我们删除。

8. 安全与跨境处理

我们采用 HTTPS 传输、iOS 钥匙串保存登录令牌等保护措施，但任何措施都无法保证绝对安全。服务商可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理信息，并应遵守适用的保护要求。

9. 变更与联系

我们可能随 App 功能变化更新本政策，并修改生效日期；法律要求时会另行通知。隐私问题或请求请联系 teng.tzx@gmail.com。
