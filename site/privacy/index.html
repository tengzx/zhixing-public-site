Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

HourNote helps you track time, manage goals and actions, and review your calendar. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, when it is shared, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: preferred language, authentication credentials, and account identifiers.
  • Sign in with Apple information: your Apple account identifier and identity token, and, depending on your choices, your name and email address.
  • Content you create: time records, event and app names, start and end times, categories, goals, actions, notes, and classification results.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens stored in the device Keychain, app preferences, and limited request data such as IP address, timestamps, and error information processed for delivery, security, and troubleshooting.
  • Notification settings: local reminder permission and schedules handled on your device.

HourNote does not use advertising SDKs, track you across other companies’ apps or websites, or sell personal information.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, provide time-tracking and planning features, keep your iPhone and paired Apple Watch consistent, process optional automatic classification when you enable it, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

3. Local storage, classification, and backup

Ordinary app content is designed to remain available locally and offline. If you enable Apple Health sleep or workout sync, HourNote reads and processes nighttime sleep summaries and workout records on your device to complete the timeline and show health details. This data is not sent to our server or third-party AI, but it is included in local backups you create or export. If you use optional Cloud AI, classification sends an event name and candidate categories through our Cloudflare Worker to the DeepSeek API. Our service does not store the original event name; the classification database stores only a one-way event-name hash, result, model version, timing and status metadata, and corrections.

HourNote creates at most one automatic local backup per day and also supports manual backups. The app keeps the latest 5 and replaces the oldest. You can download any backup to a location you choose. HourNote does not upload these backups; you are responsible for protecting and deleting downloaded copies.

4. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to provide features you choose. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, device-to-Watch system services, and App Store distribution. Cloudflare hosts authentication and optional Cloud AI services and processes limited request and security-log information. DeepSeek receives the classification data described in the consent notice only when you enable Cloud AI. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the service. We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

5. Retention

Local content and the 5 rolling backups managed by the app remain on your device until you delete them, close your account in the app, or remove the app and its data. Backups downloaded elsewhere remain until you delete them. Account information, refresh-token hashes, classification-request metadata, and classification feedback are retained while your account is active. Server-linked data is deleted when account closure succeeds, except for limited records required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or law. Cloudflare Workers operational logs are retained for no more than 7 days under current platform limits. DeepSeek processes Cloud classification inputs and outputs under its API policy.

6. Deletion and your choices

You can close your account from Preferences → Other Settings → Close Account. After server confirmation, the app removes account credentials, associated server data, and account-scoped local data. You can delete individual records, goals, and actions with their in-app controls. Deleting the app removes its local app data but does not delete backup files you previously exported elsewhere. Notification permission can be withdrawn in iOS Settings. Sign in with Apple access can be managed in Apple ID settings; revoking access does not itself delete your HourNote account. If you cannot use the in-app deletion control, contact support.

7. Children

HourNote is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the user’s jurisdiction. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security and international processing

We use safeguards including HTTPS for network transport and the iOS Keychain for authentication tokens. No security measure is perfect. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to teng.tzx@gmail.com.

Política de privacidad

Fecha de entrada en vigor: 17 de septiembre de 2026

HourNote te ayuda a registrar el tiempo, gestionar objetivos y acciones, y revisar tu calendario. Esta política explica qué información maneja la aplicación, por qué se utiliza, cuándo se comparte, durante cuánto tiempo se conserva y qué opciones tienes.

1. Información que tratamos

  • Información de la cuenta: idioma preferido, credenciales de autenticación e identificadores de cuenta.
  • Información de Iniciar sesión con Apple: el identificador de tu cuenta de Apple y el token de identidad; según tus elecciones, Apple también puede proporcionar tu nombre y correo electrónico.
  • Contenido que creas: registros de tiempo, nombres de eventos y aplicaciones, horas de inicio y finalización, categorías, objetivos, acciones, notas y resultados de clasificación.
  • Información técnica: tokens guardados en el llavero del dispositivo, preferencias y datos limitados como dirección IP, marcas de tiempo e información de errores.
  • Ajustes de notificaciones: permisos y programación de recordatorios locales en tu dispositivo.

HourNote no utiliza SDK publicitarios, no te rastrea entre aplicaciones o sitios web de otras empresas y no vende información personal.

2. Cómo utilizamos la información

Utilizamos la información para autenticar tu cuenta, ofrecer funciones de registro y planificación del tiempo, mantener sincronizados tu iPhone y el Apple Watch enlazado, procesar la clasificación automática opcional cuando la activas, evitar abusos, diagnosticar fallos, responder a solicitudes de soporte y cumplir obligaciones legales.

3. Almacenamiento local, clasificación y copias de seguridad

El contenido habitual sigue disponible localmente y sin conexión. Si activas la sincronización de sueño o entrenamientos de Apple Salud, HourNote lee y procesa en el dispositivo resúmenes del sueño nocturno y registros de entrenamiento para completar la cronología y mostrar información de salud. Estos datos no se envían al servidor ni a una IA externa, pero se incluyen en las copias locales que crees o exportes. La IA en la nube opcional envía el nombre del evento y categorías candidatas a la API de DeepSeek mediante nuestro Cloudflare Worker para clasificarlos. HourNote no guarda el nombre original del evento; para la clasificación solo conserva su hash unidireccional, el resultado, el modelo, el estado, la duración y las correcciones.

HourNote crea como máximo una copia local automática al día y también permite copias manuales. La aplicación conserva las 5 más recientes y reemplaza la más antigua. Puedes descargar cualquier copia en la ubicación que elijas. HourNote no sube estas copias; eres responsable de proteger y eliminar las que descargues.
