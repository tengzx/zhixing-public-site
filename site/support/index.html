Support

For help with Zhixing Time, email teng.tzx@gmail.com. Please include your app version, device model, iOS or watchOS version, and a description of what happened. Do not send your password or authentication tokens.

Account deletion

In the iPhone app, open Preferences → Other Settings → Close Account, then confirm. The app deletes local account-scoped data only after the server confirms deletion. If the service is temporarily unreachable, try again later or email support from the address above.

Permissions

You can change notification access at any time in iOS Settings. Sign in with Apple access is managed from Apple ID settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Response time

We aim to respond to support and privacy requests within 7 business days.

支持

如需知行使用帮助，请发送邮件至 teng.tzx@gmail.com。请附上 App 版本、设备型号、iOS 或 watchOS 版本及问题描述。请勿发送密码或登录令牌。

注销账户

在 iPhone App 中打开“偏好设置 → 其他设置 → 注销账户”并确认。只有服务器确认注销后，App 才会删除账户对应的本地数据。如果服务暂时无法访问，请稍后重试或发送邮件联系支持。

权限管理

你可以随时在 iOS 设置中修改通知权限；Apple 登录授权可在 Apple ID 设置中管理。详情请查看《隐私政策》。

回复时间

我们力争在 7 个工作日内回复支持和隐私请求。
